01 / SCOPE AND STATUS

Scope and status.

LeadMASTER is an email workspace in development. The current public preview provides informational pages and a sign-in interface. Authentication, email sending, campaigns, AI workflows and MCP integrations are not operational in the preview.

These draft terms are for review and are not presented as an effective agreement. The operator and governing law have not yet been confirmed.

02 / USE OF THE PREVIEW

Use of the preview.

Use the preview lawfully and do not interfere with its operation, attempt unauthorized access, bypass security controls, or submit harmful code. Do not enter passwords, verification codes, recipient lists or other sensitive data into the preview.

03 / FUTURE EMAIL SERVICES

Future email services.

If email sending becomes available, senders will need to use verified identities, protect their credentials and comply with applicable laws and recipient permissions. Marketing workflows require appropriate consent, unsubscribe and suppression controls; those controls are not available in the preview.

A queued message is not proof of delivery. Any future sending service will need separate, reviewed terms for deliverability, data handling, permitted use and service limits before release.

04 / AVAILABILITY AND EXTERNAL SERVICES

Availability and external services.

The preview may change, be interrupted or be withdrawn as development continues. Features shown as planned are not commitments that they are currently available. Cloudflare provides infrastructure for the preview; links to third-party services are subject to their own terms.

05 / CONTENT AND CREDENTIALS

Content and credentials.

Do not upload or send content unless you have the rights and permissions needed to use it. If future API credentials are issued, keep them on trusted servers and do not share or expose them in browser code.

06 / LEGAL REVIEW AND CONTACT

Legal review and contact.

The operator must confirm the legal entity, contact details, governing law, applicable consumer protections, and reviewed liability and dispute terms before these terms are finalized. Nothing in this draft is intended to remove rights that cannot legally be excluded.
