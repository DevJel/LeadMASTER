Data privacy.

How the current LeadMASTER preview may handle information.

01 / SCOPE AND CURRENT STATUS

Scope and current status.

LeadMASTER is an email workspace in development. The current public preview provides informational pages and a sign-in interface; sign-in, email sending, campaigns, AI workflows and MCP integrations are not operational in the preview.

This draft covers the current preview. It must be reviewed and updated before new services process personal information.

02 / INFORMATION AND PURPOSES

Information and purposes.

When you visit the site, Cloudflare may process technical information such as an IP address, request details, browser or device information and security signals to serve and protect the website. The exact logging configuration and retention periods still require confirmation.

The login screen has fields for a username or email and password, but authentication is disabled. Do not submit passwords, verification codes, recipient lists or message content to the preview.

03 / SERVICE PROVIDERS AND SECURITY

Service providers and security.

The preview is hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure. Cloudflare may process requests under its own applicable terms and privacy documentation. The login code is designed to load Cloudflare Turnstile only if its configuration becomes available; it is not currently operational in the preview.

AWS SES and Cloudflare queue and secret-storage components are part of the planned email architecture. They have not been verified as a live sending service. Their data flows, locations, retention and contractual roles must be documented before launch.

04 / COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE

Cookies and local storage.

The current site code does not set application cookies or use browser local storage. Cloudflare or future security features may use technologies needed to operate or protect the service. The operator must verify the live configuration and update this notice before relying on this statement.

05 / RETENTION AND REQUESTS

Retention and requests.

The operator must determine and document applicable retention periods, deletion procedures and any provider-held logs. Applicable law may give individuals rights to access, correct, delete or otherwise control personal information, subject to legal limitations.

A verified privacy contact and process for requests have not yet been provided. They must be added before this notice is finalized.

06 / CHANGES

Changes.

This notice must be reviewed whenever the service, its providers or its data practices change. The operator should add an effective date and an appropriate change-notification process before publication.
