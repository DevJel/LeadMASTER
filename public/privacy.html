01 / SCOPE AND CURRENT STATUS

Scope and current status.

LeadMASTER is an email workspace in development. The current public preview provides informational pages and a sign-in interface; sign-in, email sending, campaigns, AI workflows and MCP integrations are not operational in the preview.

This notice describes the categories of information that may be processed when you use LeadMASTER. It should be reviewed whenever service providers, data flows, retention periods or product features change.

02 / INFORMATION AND PURPOSES

Information and purposes.

When you visit LeadMASTER, the service and its providers may process technical information such as an IP address, request details, browser or device information and security signals to operate and protect the service. Exact logging configuration and retention periods should be confirmed by the operator.

Human administrator authentication uses credentials, security verification and an email one-time code. Do not share passwords, verification codes, API keys, recipient lists or message content in public channels.

03 / SERVICE PROVIDERS AND SECURITY

Service providers and security.

LeadMASTER uses third-party service providers to operate hosting, application security and email delivery. Those providers may process technical data or message-routing information as required to perform their services. The operator should maintain current processor agreements, locations, retention schedules and applicable privacy disclosures.

LeadMASTER processes recipient addresses, message subjects and message content to perform email submissions requested by authorized users. Delivery status and webhook metadata may also be processed. Message acceptance does not guarantee inbox delivery.

04 / COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE

Cookies and local storage.

LeadMASTER uses secure session cookies for authorized administrator access. Security and service providers may use necessary technologies to operate or protect the service. The operator must verify current cookie settings and update this notice before relying on this statement.

05 / RETENTION AND REQUESTS

Retention and requests.

The operator must determine and document applicable retention periods, deletion procedures and any provider-held logs. Applicable law may give individuals rights to access, correct, delete or otherwise control personal information, subject to legal limitations.

A verified privacy contact and process for requests have not yet been provided. They must be added before this notice is finalized.

06 / CHANGES

Changes.

This notice must be reviewed whenever the service, its providers or its data practices change. The operator should add an effective date and an appropriate change-notification process before publication.
